My Approach

How I Think About Product Photos

Simple principles behind every shoot — shaped by hands-on practice with real products.

01

Product First

Every shoot starts with the product itself — its shape, texture, materials, and best angles.

02

Light With Intent

Studio lighting shaped to flatter each material — glass, metal, fabric, food, and more.

03

Styled for Context
